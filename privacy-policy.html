1. Introduction

EkoBrix (“EkoBrix”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides AI-powered construction management software, including the EkoBrix web application and related services (collectively, the “Services”). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit ekobrix.com, create an EkoBrix account, or otherwise interact with the Services.

By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, password, company, and role.
  • Billing information — billing address, plan, and tax details. Payment card details are collected and processed by our payment processor (Stripe) and are not stored on our servers.
  • Project & customer data — plans, schedules, budgets, RFIs, drawings, documents, photos, tasks, messages, and other content you upload or generate while using the Services.
  • Communications — messages you send through our contact forms, support requests, or email.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages and features accessed, actions taken, timestamps, and referring URLs.
  • Device & log data — IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and crash logs.
  • Cookies & similar technologies — see Cookies & tracking.

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Identity providers — if you sign in with a third-party identity provider, we receive basic profile information.
  • Payments — we receive transaction status, last four digits of cards, and similar metadata from Stripe.
  • Integrations — if you connect a third-party tool (e.g., accounting or document services), we receive data you authorize that tool to share.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Services, including hosting your projects and generating dashboards, reports, and AI insights.
  • Authenticate users, secure accounts, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Process subscriptions, payments, invoices, and refunds.
  • Communicate with you about your account, transactions, product updates, and support requests.
  • Improve and develop the Services, including by analyzing usage patterns and diagnosing technical issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We rely on the following legal bases for processing personal data where required: performance of a contract, legitimate interests (such as keeping the Services secure and improving our product), your consent, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described below:

  • Within your organization — account, project, and activity data is shared with other users on your EkoBrix workspace based on the roles and permissions configured by your administrator.
  • Service providers — we use trusted vendors for hosting, analytics, error reporting, customer support, email delivery, and payment processing (including Stripe). These vendors are bound by contractual confidentiality and security obligations.
  • AI providers — to power features like Brix AI we send relevant content to language-model providers under agreements that prohibit them from training general-purpose models on your data. See AI features & data.
  • Legal & safety — we may disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect rights, property, or safety.
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to confidentiality.
  • With your consent — in any other case where you direct us to share information.

5. AI features & data

The Services include AI-powered features (such as Brix AI) that generate summaries, reports, forecasts, and answers based on your project content. To deliver these features, we may transmit relevant project content to third-party AI providers operating under contractual restrictions.

We do not allow third-party AI providers to use your customer data to train their general-purpose foundation models. AI outputs are generated based on probabilistic models and may contain errors; you should review them before relying on them for critical decisions.

6. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, measure how the Services are used, and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking essential cookies may prevent the Services from functioning properly.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services and comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer needed we delete or anonymize it. Account administrators can request export or deletion of project data at any time.

8. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls, audit logging, and regular review of our security practices. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International transfers

EkoBrix operates internationally. Your information may be processed in countries other than the one in which you reside. Where required by applicable law, we implement appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) to protect personal data when it is transferred internationally.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at arik@ekobrix.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

11. Children’s privacy

The Services are not directed to children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the “Effective date” above and, where appropriate, by email or in-product notice. Your continued use of the Services after the changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at arik@ekobrix.com.
